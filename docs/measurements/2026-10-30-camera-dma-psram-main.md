# Camera DMA channel to pinned PSRAM (2026-10-30)

Base `d824feaa8acc66dc16810fc9fa95887f5efc642d` plus the uncommitted camera integration worktree. The explicit default-off `:camera-dma-psram` fixture pins a 921,600-byte task-owned PSRAM frame and prepares its cache in bounded chunks. It acquires camera DMA channel 1, marks the frame and channel as started, then copies a known 64-byte pattern from dedicated SRAM to the aligned beginning of the frame. Success requires the transfer completion event, observed channel disable and empty FIFO, no channel/common error, cache invalidation and matching 64 bytes. The whole frame and started channel remain pinned until reset on both success and failure; subsequent camera diagnostics are refused.

One Tab5 ESP32-P4 rev 1.3 physical trial, MAC `e8:f6:0a:e2:e8:9c`: application SHA-256 `35dffb709f856030905f0629b1eeb519ac0be7727746a4972bf9a46fd3329eae`, ELF SHA-256 `f0b1cc7089f813cc4f38dbd508cfd72e790d1d77609fd5ab84d9ba91bd7ce376`. [Receipt and image audit](evidence/2026-10-30-camera-dma-psram-main/). Explicit request kind 13 was accepted; preparation was observed, followed by two repeated state 4/result 1 reports for the same single transfer. Launcher readiness and display frame and CPU counter progress were recorded.

The initial attempt stopped before opening or writing the device because another process held serial; after the port became free, the successful trial used the shared lock and exclusive serial access. The transaction restored the then-existing loader and app spans (24,576 and 155,648 bytes), verified their full readback, and issued a hard reset; restored-system boot was not captured. Raw serial logs remain private.

The host replay covers the generic transfer implementation's success, corruption, timeout and fault paths; host workspace tests cover pinned lifecycle. Debug host tests and the diagnostic native image audit passed. This is a 64-byte SRAM-to-PSRAM transfer, not a full-frame transfer, a camera image, video, independent optical proof, or evidence that a started channel/frame is safe to reuse.
