# Full-frame scan negative control (2026-10-30)

On base `d824feaa8acc66dc16810fc9fa95887f5efc642d` with the uncommitted camera integration, the opt-in `:camera-scan-control` command uses the same normally completed, pinned 921,600-byte CSI frame and two-pass scan as `:camera-frame-scan`. After the first pass and the timer gap, it flips one byte in the final 1,024-byte chunk, with the existing frame identity, channel ownership, fault and stopped-state checks. It then scans the frame again. The channel and frame remain retained until reset; this diagnostic does not release them.

One physical Tab5 ESP32-P4 rev 1.3 trial, MAC `e8:f6:0a:e2:e8:9c`, reported 900 chunks, exactly one changed chunk, a SHA-256 mismatch and successful injection. The two terminal reports describe one comparison of one frame. Display and CPU counters progressed. This validates detection of this deliberate single-byte change on this candidate; it does not establish that every possible corruption will be detected or that the underlying DMA and IRQ paths have retired.

App SHA-256 `b2e13358b262025f78c482e3ecb862d3469d08919edf422f665707367a4e99a7`; ELF SHA-256 `57ed3168cd94facc3b6fc59bf9fe614a7ebbfd568938fa691cc4ab21a44aca48`. The diagnostic native build passed the existing image audit and SRAM floor, with network and normal storage disabled. The host command parser and stability tests passed, including deliberate mutation and comparison against the standard SHA-256 implementation. The qualification script was a private adaptation of the earlier camera qualifier: it sends the new command, admits a negative control on a completed frame, and requires request kind 25, one changed chunk, SHA mismatch and injection. Its flash transaction and board identity checks were unchanged; the adapted scripts are in `/private/tmp/nihos-camera-scan-control-tools/tools`.

The exact candidate was verified before and after capture; both previous loader and app erase spans were restored and their full readback verified, followed by a hard reset. No restored-system boot was captured. The [receipt and image audit](evidence/2026-10-30-camera-frame-scan-control/) contain no image pixels; raw serial and backups remain outside the repository. Optical correctness, DMA/frame reuse, repeated captures and video remain unqualified.
