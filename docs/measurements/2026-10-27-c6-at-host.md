The new host tests cover passive receive announcements; binary TCP bytes
containing NUL/CRLF/OK/ERROR/prompts; interleaved BLE notifications and socket
read responses; indication metadata; prompt arming; terminal-error cleanup;
maximum payloads; malformed and unsupported binary headers; integer overflow;
line limits and UTF-8 byte preservation; truncated-transfer abort; timeout and
late-completion rejection; session and request token freshness; and counter
exhaustion without wrap.

Inputs are synthetic streams based on the pinned upstream protocol. They are
not C6 captures and do not establish firmware conformance. The gate is not
yet wired to an AT command controller. The compile probe exercises production
decoder/gate methods on RV32 but is not linked into the P4 application.

## Remaining gates

C6 firmware build/config resolution, P4 SDIO driver, AT controller and resource
tables, recovery/backup access, hardware flashing, real TCP/BLE concurrency,
microSD/display interference, memory/latency/loss measurements, HTTPS trust
validation and BLE HID remain open. No serial port was opened and no device
image or OTA selection was changed in this work.
